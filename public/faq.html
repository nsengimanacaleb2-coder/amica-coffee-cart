FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking at least 2–3 weeks ahead, especially for weekend weddings and large corporate events, since carts get booked up.
Do you travel outside Kigali? +
Yes — enter your event location on the booking form and we'll confirm travel details and any additional travel fee.
What happens after I submit a booking request? +
Your request goes to Pending. We review cart availability and confirm within 24–48 hours — you'll see the status update in your dashboard and get an email.
Can I change my event date after booking? +
Yes, as long as your booking is still Pending — use the Reschedule option in your dashboard. Already-approved bookings should be changed by contacting us directly.
What payment methods do you accept? +
Cash on the day, Mobile Money, or Bank Transfer. Online card payment is available on select approved bookings.
Do you provide cups, sugar, and milk options? +
Yes — every cart comes fully stocked, including dairy-free milk options on request.
How many guests can one cart serve? +
It depends on the cart — the Classic cart handles up to 50 guests, Premium up to 150, and Deluxe up to 300. Check each cart's details page for capacity.
